The Unit Circle and the Trigonometric Ratios
On the unit circle

Off, the end of the stick is labelled (x, y). On, it is labelled with what x and y are on the unit circle.

Past 90°: the acute angle the stick makes with the x-axis, the same triangle drawn faintly in the first quadrant, and sin 150° = sin 30° in the readout.

On the whole circle: which ratios are positive in each quadrant, in the corners of the board and as shaded bands on the graphs.

The stick is extended to meet the line x = 1; the green height there is tan θ.

Numbers

At special angles the numbers are written exactly (½, √3 ⁄ 2, 1 ⁄ √2). Off, every value is a decimal to 2 places.
