Area of a Circle
The story

Rectangle adds one step: the slice at one end is cut in half and the half moves to the other end, so both ends stand up straight. Parallelogram stops when the two halves fit together — base × height, as in the parallelogram tool.

On screen

On: the radius is r and the rim halves are πr, and the story ends on πr². Off: a number for the radius (set it in the side panel), and the area comes out as a number.

The dashed outline appears once the sides are measured. “How close” is a side-panel card that measures the bumpy shape as the slices get thinner.
