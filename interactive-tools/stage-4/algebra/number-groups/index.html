Collecting Like Terms – Number Groups
New questions

Letters keeps the same counts: 3 × 7 + 2 × 7 becomes 3x + 2x. Same as the side panel’s Numbers / Letters switch (or L).

Big groups make the point: 3 × 38 + 2 × 38 + 5 × 38 is easy once you count the groups first.

One term is subtracted, e.g. 3x + 8x − 2x: two groups come off the pile.

One term is groups of something else (2 × 5, or 2y). Its bars are a different size, so they can’t join the pile.

On screen

A group of 7 is drawn as 7 little squares. Turn it off and a group of 7 looks just like a group of x — which is the point.

Adds a last step: give x a value and the same picture becomes a numbers question with the same answer both ways.
