Collecting Like Terms – Bubbles
New questions

A −x and an x make a zero pair: drop one on the other and both pop.

A number on its own is a pile of ones: 4 is four 1s. Ones only join ones.

ab and ba are like terms (they join, and the bas turn into abs). x and x² are not.

On the board

Off by default, so the class has to read each bubble to find its partners.

Drop one x on empty space and 5x becomes 4x + x — the same amount, written another way.
