How to play

  • Use every number from 1 to 16 exactly once (1 to 9 on a 3 × 3 board). Some are already placed for you.
  • Each row is read left to right, and each column top to bottom, with the signs in the circles between the squares.
  • Every row and every column has to work out to the target.
  • The order of operations applies. Do all the × and ÷ first, left to right, then the + and −, left to right.
    3 + 4 × 2 = 3 + 8 = 11  — not 14.
    12 ÷ 4 × 3 = 3 × 3 = 9  — not 1.
  • Tap a number and then a square, or drag a number onto a square. Drag a number off the board, or tap it twice, to take it back. On a keyboard: arrows to move, type the number, Backspace to clear.
  • Any arrangement that makes every line hit the target counts as solved.
