Crack the Equation

Students find a hidden equation in six guesses. The catch: every guess has to be a true equation. Demo it online, then pairs play on paper.

⏱ 40–45 minutes👥 Whole class, then pairs🎯 Fluency · order of operations🎓 Years 5–10
24+18=42
15+27=42

🎯 Learning intention

Use clues and reasoning to find a hidden equation, checking every step is true.

✅ Success criteria

  • I can write a true equation that fills an exact number of tiles.
  • I can check a guess is true, doing × and ÷ before + and −.
  • I can use the clues to make my next guess smarter.

Lesson flow · about 40 minutes

1
Hook3 min
  • Open the game on the board. Show the empty tiles.
Say“An equation is hiding here. Every guess must be true. Can we crack it in six?”
2
Demo togetherOnline10 min
  • Practice mode, Sweet or Mild. Students call out guesses; you type.
  • After each guess, read the colours together: what do they tell us?
  • Type one false guess on purpose. The game explains why it isn’t true.
Ask“Where could the = go?” · “What do the grey tiles rule out?”
3
Show the paper version5 min
  • Hand out the sheets. Code-maker: writes a secret on the top strip, checks it’s true, cuts it off, keeps it hidden.
  • Model marking one guess with the key on the right.
4
Pairs playPaper15–20 min
  • Code-breaker writes a guess and shows it’s true. Partner marks every tile.
  • Swap jobs after each game.
  • You: check strips are true before they’re cut.
5
Wrap up5 min
Ask“What was your best first guess? Why?” · “Who caught a guess that wasn’t true?”

📦 Before the lesson

  • Print the sheet: game → Tools → Worksheet pairs creator. One per student.
  • Scissors and pencils for each pair.
  • Game open on the board: millsmathstools.au → Crack the Equation.
