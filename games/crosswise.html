      How to play

      Equation crossword

      • Each row and each column of boxes is one equation: number, sign, number, =, answer. Equations cross where they share a number.
      • Fill every dashed box so that EVERY equation is true. Start with an equation that has only one box missing — the number you find there helps the equation that crosses it.
      • Each = sign turns green when its equation is complete and true, and red when it is not.
      • Spicy and Extra Spicy use negative numbers. At Extra Spicy some signs are missing too — work out whether it is +, −, × or ÷.
      • Stuck on Sweet or Mild? The Number bank shows the missing numbers. Tap one to put it in the selected box.

      Cross-number

      • It is a crossword, but every answer is a number with one digit in each square. No answer starts with 0, and no two answers are the same.
      • Tap a square to choose it, and tap it again to swap between Across and Down. The clue you are on is shown above the grid.
      • Some clues have many possible answers — "a square number", "a multiple of 7". Use the digits from the answers that cross it to decide which one it is.
      • Some clues use another answer: "3 Down + 12" means add 12 to the answer to 3 Down.
      • Check marks each finished answer with ✓ or ✗.
