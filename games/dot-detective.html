    How to play

    • Find the Shape. Some points on the grid are marked with dots. Exactly one set of them are the corners of the shape named. Tap them — the order does not matter.
    • Shapes can be tilted. A square does not have to sit flat: count across and up along each side to check the sides are equal and the corners are right angles.
    • Names mean what the definitions say. A square is also a rectangle, a rhombus and a parallelogram, so a “rectangle” puzzle never has a square hidden in it as well — there is only ever one answer.
    • Find the Triangle. Choose three dots that make a triangle with exactly the area given. Each grid square is 1 square unit.
    • For a sloping triangle, draw the box around it, then take away the right-angled triangles in the corners. That is the box method, and the game will show it to you.
    • Tap a dot again to take it back. Keyboard: Tab to a dot, Enter or Space to choose it.
