How to play

  • Guess the hidden equation in six tries. Each guess fills every tile and must be a true equation — so every guess is also a calculation.
  • After a guess the tiles change colour:
    1
    2
    +
    4
    ×
    3
    =
    2
    Green: right symbol, right place. Amber: in the equation but in a different place. Grey: not in the equation, or you have used it more times than it appears.
  • Order of operations counts. 3 + 4 × 5 is 23, not 35, because × and ÷ are done first. If a guess is not true, the game shows you the working so you can see why.
  • The right of the = is only ever a number. Numbers never start with 0, and the hidden answer is always a whole number.
  • Numbers can be in either order: 3 + 4 = 7 and 4 + 3 = 7 are different guesses.
  • Levels: 🍬 Sweet 6 tiles, + and − · 🍃 Mild 7 tiles, one operation · 🌶️ Medium 8 tiles · 🔥 Spicy 8 tiles, always two operations where the order matters · 🌋 Extra Spicy 10 tiles.
  • Keyboard: type digits and + − * / (or x for ×) and =, Enter to guess, Backspace to delete, ← → to move. Click a tile to jump to it.
