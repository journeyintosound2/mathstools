How to play

  • Start in the top-left square (the arrow) and finish on the target in the bottom-right corner.
  • Move one square at a time — up, down, left or right, never diagonally — and never use the same square twice. You reach the target through one of the two arrow squares next to it.
  • Keep a running total. The first number is where the total starts. Each sign and the number after it change the total straight away, in the order you walk them.
    5 → +8 → 13 → ×7 → 91 → −8 → 83
  • This is not one long sum. Each step is worked on its own, so the order of operations does not come into it. If you wrote the same trail as a single sum, 5 + 8 × 7 − 8, the order of operations would make it 53 — a different answer. That is why the trail is never written that way.
  • Every step has to give a whole number, so you cannot step onto a number that does not divide the total exactly.
  • Every board has a set number of trails that finish on the target. Find them all.
  • Drag from the end of your trail, or tap the next square. Tap a square already on your trail to go back to it. Keyboard: arrows to move, Backspace to step back, Esc to clear.
