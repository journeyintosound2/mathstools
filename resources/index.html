About this page. Outcome codes and statements are taken verbatim from the NSW Mathematics K–10 Syllabus (2022), © NSW Education Standards Authority. Focus areas that split across A / B / C / D are collated into a single topic here. Stage 4 and Stage 5 outcomes keep their own Core or Path badge, with NESA's pathway note (Stn = Standard, Adv = Advanced, Ext = Extension). Stage 3 carries no badge — Part A and Part B are a Year 5 / Year 6 emphasis of the same outcomes, and K–6 has no Core/Path distinction.
