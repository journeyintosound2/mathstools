About this page. Outcome codes and statements are taken verbatim from the NSW Mathematics K–10 Syllabus (2022), © NSW Education Standards Authority. Focus areas that split across A / B / C / D are collated into a single topic here. Stage 4 and Stage 5 outcomes keep their own Core or Path badge, with NESA's pathway note (Stn = Standard, Adv = Advanced, Ext = Extension). Stage 3 carries no badge — Part A and Part B are a Year 5 / Year 6 emphasis of the same outcomes, and K–6 has no Core/Path distinction; the small A or B beside a Stage 3 content statement says which part it comes from.

The content statements are NESA's, word for word, as listed in its Record of learning and achievement using syllabus content documents for Stages 3–5. Where a Stage 3 focus area lists several outcomes together, each statement sits under the outcome it serves. A resource that spans a whole outcome rather than one idea is listed under Across the whole outcome.
