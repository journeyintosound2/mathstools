# Area of a Trapezium — Worksheet Generator (2026-11-16)

`worksheet-creators/stage-4/measurement-space/area-of-a-trapezium.html` — single file on the Area of a Parallelogram generator's frame (bands, level tick-chips, lettered sections with counts, rows measured then packed into A4, "Your turn" box in leftover space, answer key). Listed under MA4-ARE-C-01, homepage Worksheet mini card, the teaching tool's Tools menu.

## Sections (the lesson order)
- **A Average base** (default 2) — the cuts at the middles, curved swing arrows and the dashed rectangle are drawn. Student fills `( __ + __ ) ÷ 2 = __` then `Area = __ × __ = __`.
- **B Half a parallelogram** (2) — the turned copy is drawn dashed. Base `__ + __`, parallelogram `__ × __`, trapezium `__ ÷ 2`.
- **C Halve the height** (2; added later on 2026-11-16) — the dotted cut halfway up, the turning point, a curved arrow and the dashed piece where the top half lands (with its a) are drawn. Base `__ + __`, height `__ ÷ 2`, area `__ × __`. Even heights on Sweet and Mild.
- **D Find the area** (4) — kinds mixed, distractors and turned shapes as chosen.
- **E Work backwards** (2) — area given; height missing (from Medium also a parallel side). Always whole answers.
- **F Spot the mistake** (1, full width) — forgot to halve / halved twice / used the sloping side / multiplied the parallel sides / used only one side.
- **G Draw your own** (1) — a given area on squares, two different ways (or right-angled + leaning, or same height).
- **H Fluency** (off, 12) — no diagram; some "find the height" from Medium.

The printed letters come from `LT`. Internally the new section is key `M`, and the old keys (C–G) are unchanged.

## Teacher options
- **Levels** as tick-chips (one = every question that size; several = a ladder through each section): Sweet (to 10, whole averages), Mild (to 12), Medium (to 14, some halves), Spicy (to 20, more halves). Quick sets.
- **Kinds of trapezium:** isosceles, right-angled, leaning, neither (one always stays on).
- **Sloping side labelled:** never / sometimes / often (whole-number triples, so the height has to be chosen).
- **Turned on its side:** never / some / most (sections D–F only).
- Unit squares (upright, whole, big enough), answer space in D (answer line or working lines), units.
- **Worked example:** average base, half a parallelogram, halve the height, average base + two copies, or **all three (default)**. With all three, the example prints as three columns, each a picture over one line of working. A "show the formula" toggle lets a class still discovering it see only the pictures.
- Spacing (spacious / standard / compact), colour or black and white, name/date, answer key (every answer with a one-line reason).

Settings that change WHAT is asked re-roll; looks-only settings re-lay the same questions. Test surface `window.__WS__`.
