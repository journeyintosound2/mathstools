## Purpose
Show that a unit fraction of an amount IS a division (1/5 of 20 = 20 ÷ 5), and that any other fraction is that division done a times: 2/5 of 20 = 2 × 1/5 of 20 = 2 × (20 ÷ 5) = (20 ÷ 5) × 2 = 20/5 × 2.

## The story (→ / Enter, ← back)
1. The whole: one bar with the amount written in it.
2. Cut: b equal parts, each "?" and named 1/b; the amount lifts into a bracket above the bar.
3. One part: each part shows n ÷ b. The record writes 1/b of n = n ÷ b = v.
4. Take (only when a > 1): a parts turn orange; "2 fifths".
5. Answer: bracket under the taken parts reads a × v = answer, and a copy flies into the sentence.

## Design decisions
- Colour is the role in every form: amount blue, denominator / ÷ purple, numerator / × orange, one part teal, answer green.
- "Write it as" switch, five forms: 2/5 of 20 · 2 × 1/5 of 20 (that many groups of one part) · 2 × (20 ÷ 5) · (20 ÷ 5) × 2 · 20/5 × 2 (the division written as a fraction; the fraction line groups it, so no brackets). Each number flies to its new place. In the four non-fraction forms the one-part piece (1/5 of 20, the bracket, or 20/5) shows its value underneath from step 3, which makes the order of operations visible.
- For a unit fraction, 1 × 1/5 of 20 is just 1/5 of 20 and the two bracket forms are both 20 ÷ 5, so only three forms show.
- On the board the form buttons are compact, the "Write it as" label hides, and "Your own" shows as its icon below 1400px, so the row stays one line. The focus view keeps the label and big buttons.
- Keys: 1–5 pick a form, T cycles, Space = New question, A = new amount, F = focus view, O = ones.
- Focus view (corner icon or F): the sentence card morphs to the centre over a dark veil so the class looks only at the forms; ← / → cycle the forms there. Esc, F, the icon or a click on the veil morphs it back.
- Panel buttons: New amount · New question · Your own (Your own hides in the focus view).
- Stage 3 default fractions: halves, quarters, fifths, tenths (the syllabus list). Options add thirds/sixths/eighths, or any 2–12.
- One part is kept to a tables fact (2–10) by default, or 6–25; amount ≤ 300.
- Ones view (O): faint unit lines in each part, amounts up to 120.
- Optional: check (parts rebuild the whole), and the other order (multiply first: 2 × 20 ÷ 5).
