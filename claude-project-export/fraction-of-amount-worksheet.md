# Fraction of an Amount — Worksheet Creator (built 2026-11-03)

Generator: `worksheet-creators/stage-3/number/fraction-of-amount-division.html`
Partner of the teaching tool `interactive-tools/stage-3/number/fraction-of-amount-division/` (first row of its Tools menu) and the Student Quiz `online-quizzes/stage-3/number/fraction-of-amount-division.html` (linked from the intro; see `claude/fraction-of-amount-quiz.md`).

Built on the Area of a Parallelogram generator's frame: setting bands, level chips, lettered problem types with counts, rows measured then packed into A4 pages, a "Your turn" box in leftover space, answer key pages.

## The idea
The bar comes off one step at a time until the notation carries the idea on its own.

## Settings
- **Levels set the size of one part** (the division a student does): Sweet 2–5, Mild to 10, Medium 6–12, Spicy 11–25. Amount ≤ 300. Default Sweet + Mild.
- **Fractions:** halves, quarters, fifths, tenths (Stage 3, default) · + thirds, sixths, eighths · any to twelfths. Random fractions are in simplest form.
- Name each part (⅕), show the ones in the bars, room to draw a bar in word problems.
- Section D as "fill the gaps" or "write each form on a line".
- Spacing, colour or black and white, worked example (with or without the rule "divide by the bottom number, multiply by the top"), name and date, answer key.

## Sections (counts editable)
- **A One part is a division:** unit fractions; bar already cut; fill each part, then ⅕ of 20 = 20 ÷ __ = __.
- **B Divide, then multiply:** bar cut and shaded; one part, then the shaded parts.
- **C Draw the bar:** an uncut bar with the amount; cut, shade, then a × (n ÷ b).
- **D Write it five ways:** from the fraction to a × 1/b of n, a × (n ÷ b), (n ÷ b) × a and n/b × a; and back from a form (including a × 1/b of n) to the fraction it comes from.
- **E True or false:** two true (including a × 1/b of n, n ÷ b × a and (a × n) ÷ b) and two false (swapped top and bottom, one part only, never divided), then fix one.
- **F Spot the mistake:** swapped, stopped at one part, never divided, or added the top number.
- **G Use it:** 12 word-problem contexts.
- **H Build the whole:** "⅗ of a number is 18" — one part, then the whole (MA3-RQF-02 "build up to the whole").
- **I Fluency:** off by default; all five forms, three a row.

The worked example's SO line reads ⅖ of 20 = 2 × ⅕ of 20 = 2 × (20 ÷ 5) = 8.

## Design rules
- Gaps are ruled lines, never boxes. A fraction to write in is just the fraction line with room above and below.
- Colours match the tool: amount blue, number of parts purple, parts taken orange. True/false and spot-the-mistake statements are plain ink so colour can't give the answer away.
