# Visual Patterns (Stage 4, MA4-LIN-C-01)

- Teaching tool: `interactive-tools/stage-4/algebra/visual-patterns/index.html`
- Shared pattern library: `interactive-tools/stage-4/algebra/visual-patterns/patterns.js` (window.MMT_VP — used by the tool AND the worksheet)
- Worksheet creator: `worksheet-creators/stage-4/algebra/visual-patterns.html`

Built 2026-10-16 from Jeff's "Visual Patterns" notice/wonder deck and his visual patterns worksheet, in the spirit of visualpatterns.org (all figures drawn fresh as SVG).

## Purpose
A lead-in to linear relationships: capture curiosity, predict, generalise. **No tables of values or rules on the tool's screen** — those go on the teacher's whiteboard (or the worksheet).

## Lesson flow in the tool (Next / Back)
1. Figure 1 alone — notice / wonder (or figures 1–3 together, setting)
2. Figure 2 — what changed?  3. Figure 3 — how do you see it growing?
4. "?" — predict and sketch figure 4 → built from figure 3 (new pieces pop in orange)
5. Figure 0 (setting): figure 1 loses one step of growth; "Empty" or "Can't be drawn" where relevant
6. Far figures (10, 30, 100 by default; presets 10/20/50, 10/43/100, 5/10/20): "?" then it grows step by step until "Too big to draw"
7. Work backwards (setting): which figure has c pieces?
8. Describe any figure *n*

## Board tools
How do you see it? (recolour by way of seeing) · What's new · Counts · Tap to count · Pen · Full screen (F) · Pattern gallery (G) · Random (R, stays within linear or challenge)

## Patterns — 57
- **Linear (42):** Sweet 11, Mild 14, Medium 14, Spicy 3 — dots, tiles, matchsticks, cubes and tables (trapezium, square, triangle, hexagon). Rules range from 2n to 6n + 1, constants up to + 8, and negatives (2n − 1, 4n − 3).
- **Challenge — not linear (15):** staircase, growing squares, rectangles n(n+1), step pyramid, diamond, square with a tail, square missing a corner, Two blocks (2n² + 2n + 3, adapted from visualpatterns.org, CC BY 4.0), triangle numbers, hexagon numbers, matchstick grid, triangle of matchstick triangles, cube of cubes, cube pyramid, doubling.
- **Tables restyled** (the teal/yellow trapezium look is Make Math Moments' design): light-blue tables, brown square seats.

## Worksheet creator
- Level ladder or hand-picked patterns (thumbnail picker, in order); 1–8 linear + 0–2 challenge at the end.
- Parts (each optional, lesson order): describe in words · draw figure 4 · draw figure 0 · table (1–5 / 0–5 / 1–5 & 10 / 1–5 & n) · predict (a different figure 6–15 per question, or 10/20/100) · work backwards · rule (open / "___ × figure number + ___" / words then symbols) · show how you see it.
- Dot paper in drawing boxes (isometric for cube patterns); black and white unless colour chosen; name/date; answer key that draws figures 4 and 0, fills the table and gives the rule plus one way of seeing it.
- Questions are never split across pages (Jeff's .docx split Question 3).
