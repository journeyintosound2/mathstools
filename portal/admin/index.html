Growth

Are students getting better, and are they taking on harder work? Scores and spice levels over time, for any class, teacher or topic.

Task history isn't readable yet. Publish the updated Firestore rules (the admin achievements read rule) in the Firebase Console to light up Growth.

Average score over time

Mean percent per bucket. Read it alongside the level mix — a class moving up a rung will dip here.

Difficulty-weighted score

Percent × spice rung. Rises when students score better or take on harder work, so it doesn't punish climbing.

Level mix

Share of attempts at each spice level. Warm colours rising over time means the ladder is being climbed.

Student growth

Like-for-like gain compares a student's first and latest attempt on the same topic at the same level, so difficulty is held constant. Students with only one attempt per topic show “—”.
