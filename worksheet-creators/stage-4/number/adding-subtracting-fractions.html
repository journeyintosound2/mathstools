Adding and Subtracting Fractions — Worksheet Generator

Printable A4 worksheets built from the Adding and Subtracting Fractions teaching tool. The levels are the tool's own ladder: same denominators (count the pieces), related (one fraction renames), unrelated (both rename), and Extra hot — non-related (coprime) denominators up to 10, where the new unit is the denominators multiplied. With Show the model on, each question prints the two fraction bars and a number line already cut into the common unit, with the starting fraction marked — students count on or back. Worksheet language prints every instruction bilingually — Arabic or Farsi on top, English beneath — for EAL/D students; the maths stays in Western numerals.
