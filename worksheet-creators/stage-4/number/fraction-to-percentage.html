Fraction to Percentage — Worksheet Generator

Printable A4 worksheets built from the Fraction to Percentage teaching tool. The levels are the tool's own three tiers. With Show number line on, every question arrives at the tool's first reveal — the whole partitioned into equal parts with the fraction marked — so students count on in percent. With it off you get a plain conversion card.
