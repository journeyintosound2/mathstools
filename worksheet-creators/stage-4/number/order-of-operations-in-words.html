Order of Operations — Say What You See — Worksheet Generator

Printable A4 worksheets from the Order of Operations — Say What You See teaching tool. No acronym: every number sentence is read as words and drawn as tiles — 5 + 3 × 5 is 5 and 3 groups of 5, and 10 − 4 ÷ 2 is 10 take away half of 4. The pictures are the board’s own.

The paper

Spacious is made for younger hands: big print, big tiles and tall writing lines. In black and white the tiles are outlined, a negative tile is grey, a tile being taken away is crossed out, and a faded part is dashed.

The order is the lesson: see it, say it, write it, draw it, work it out, then argue about it. G Fluency is off until you tick it: a block of number sentences with no pictures — on its own it makes a quick warm-up page. Every section climbs its own ladder from its first question to its last.

Options

Tile diagrams put the picture beside the symbols in B, C, E and F — the scaffold. Turn them off once a class can picture it for themselves; A is a picture by definition and D asks the student to draw it. The strip prints the signs as words at the top of page 1: + and, − take away, × groups of, ÷ half of, a third of…, ( ) one amount. The answer key draws every picture and writes every sentence in words.
