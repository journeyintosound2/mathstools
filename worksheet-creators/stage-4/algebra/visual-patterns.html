Visual Patterns — Worksheet Generator

Printable A4 worksheets from the Visual Patterns teaching tool — the same patterns, drawn the same way. Each question shows figures 1, 2 and 3; you choose what the student does with them: put the change into words, draw the next figure and figure 0, fill in a table, predict, work backwards and write the rule.

Colour or circle the groups on the figures.

The parts print in the order a lesson runs: notice it, draw what comes next, go back to figure 0, organise it in a table, predict a far figure, work backwards, then generalise with a rule. The challenge patterns get the same questions; their rule line becomes “Can you find a rule?”, since none of them is ___ × n + ___.

Options

Figures print in black and white unless you choose colour — they photocopy cleanly. Cube patterns get isometric dot paper in their drawing boxes, everything else a square dot grid. The answer key draws figure 4 and figure 0, fills the table, and gives the rule with one way of seeing it in words.
