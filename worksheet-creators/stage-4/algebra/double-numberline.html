One-Step Equations — Double Number Line — Worksheet Generator

Printable A4 worksheets from the Double Number Line teaching tool (and its Student Quiz). On a double number line each expression sits above its value. To solve, make the move that gets back to x — and make it on both lines. The line is printed in the first sections, students draw their own in the third, and from then on the picture is in their heads.

The numbers — tick one level, or several for a ladder

Quick sets:

One level ticked: every question is that size. Several ticked: each section climbs through them, easiest first. Sweet: small whole numbers, written x + 5 = 12. Mild: bigger whole numbers. Medium: negative numbers. Spicy: bigger negatives, and numbers up to 12 in front of x.

How the equations are written

On the printed lines orange is what was done to x and purple is the move that undoes it — the same colours as the teaching tool. In black and white the build move is dashed.

The order is the lesson, and the picture fades: read the line (going forward), undo it on the line with the line printed, draw the line yourself, then solve, solve and check, write the equation from words, use it, spot the mistake and make your own. Scaffolding and the paper

The worked example draws one equation on a double number line with the undo move on both lines, then writes it out and checks it. The answer key fills in every line and gives a one-line reason, so it can be handed to a student.
