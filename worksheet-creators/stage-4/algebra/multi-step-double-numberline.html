Printable A4 worksheets from the Multi-Step Equations teaching tool. An equation is one point on a double number line — the left side on top, the right side underneath — and solving is the same move on both lines, until x is on its own. The first move is printed on the line in section A, the working is laid out = under = in section B, students draw their own in C, and from D on the picture is in their heads.
