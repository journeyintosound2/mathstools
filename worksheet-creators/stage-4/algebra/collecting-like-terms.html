Collecting Like Terms – Bubbles — Worksheet Generator

Printable A4 worksheets from the Bubbles teaching tool (and its Student Quiz). Each term is a bubble of pieces: like terms hold the same thing, so their bubbles join, and a piece and its opposite make a zero pair. The bubbles are printed in the first sections, students draw their own in the third, and from then on the picture is in their heads.

The numbers — tick one level, or several for a ladder

Quick sets:

One level ticked: every question is that size. Several ticked: each section climbs through them, easiest first. Sweet: two kinds of term, all added. Mild: one term taken away. Medium: three kinds, two taken away, a kind can cancel. Spicy: three kinds in six terms, and answers below zero.

How it is written

The count each kind first line under a question gives one short line per kind of term (x, y, ones) before the answer — the bubble picture, written down. Switch it to answer only when the class no longer needs it. In black and white a taken-away bubble is dashed.

Problem types — tick the ones you want, and how many

On the paper:0 questions

The order is the lesson, and the picture fades: join the bubbles and zero pairs with the bubbles printed, draw the bubbles yourself, then collect, take away, plain numbers, groups of numbers (collecting saves multiplications), look-alikes, missing terms, spot the mistake and make your own. L Fluency is off until you tick it.

Scaffolding and the paper

The worked example draws 3x + 2y + 4x − y as bubbles and the two bubbles they collect into. The answer key gives every answer with a one-line reason (how many of each kind), so it can be handed to a student.
