Area of a Circle — Worksheet Generator

Printable A4 worksheets from the Area of a Circle teaching tool. Slice a circle, fit the slices together and they make a rectangle half the circumference long and the radius high: πr × r = πr². Then radius, diameter, parts of a circle, working backwards, mistakes, real problems and shaded regions.

The order is the lesson: slices to a rectangle (why it is πr²), radius, diameter, parts of a circle, work backwards, spot the mistake, then problems in context. H Shaded regions (composite areas) and I Fluency are off until you tick them.

Scaffolding and the paper

Turn the formula off while the class is still discovering it — the example then shows the slices as a rectangle, half the circumference by the radius, without “A = πr²”. Changing π, rounding, units or the answer space keeps the same questions, so you can print one set with π ≈ 3 and an identical set in exact form for different groups. The answer key shows the working, so it can be handed to a student.
