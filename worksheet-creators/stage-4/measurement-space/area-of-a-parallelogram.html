Area of a Parallelogram & Rhombus — Worksheet Generator

Printable A4 worksheets from the Area of a Parallelogram & Rhombus teaching tool (and its Student Quiz). Every area comes back to a rectangle: rows of squares, a parallelogram cut and slid into a base × height rectangle, and a rhombus that is half the rectangle round its diagonals.

The numbers — tick one level, or several for a ladder

Quick sets:

One level ticked: every question is that size. Several ticked: each section climbs through them, easiest first. Spicy uses lengths in halves (6.5) when finding an area; working backwards always comes out to whole numbers.

The shapes

The shape switches decide what sections C, E, F and G mix together (at least one stays on); A is always rectangles, B parallelograms and D rhombuses by their diagonals. A sloping side is labelled with a whole-number length (3-4-5, 6-8-10, 5-12-13 …) so students must pick the length at right angles to the base. Unit squares print only when the lengths are whole numbers and the squares are big enough to count; section A always has them.

On the paper:0 questions

The order is the lesson: rows of squares, cut and slide a parallelogram into a rectangle, find areas, a rhombus as half the rectangle round its diagonals, work backwards, spot the mistake, then draw your own. H Fluency is off until you tick it: bare questions with no diagram, good as a warm-up page on its own.

Scaffolding and the paper

Turn the formulas off when the class is still discovering them — the example then shows the cut and slide and the rectangle it makes, without “base × height”. The answer key fills in every answer with a one-line reason, so it can be handed to a student.
