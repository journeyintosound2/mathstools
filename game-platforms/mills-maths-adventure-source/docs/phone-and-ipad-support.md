# Phone + iPad support (Mills Maths Adventure)

*Built 2026-10-11. Standing rule from Jeff: every future challenge, world or update must work on iPhone, iPad and small screens, not only on a computer.*

## How students play on a phone or iPad

| To… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Walk | Put your left thumb anywhere in the lower-left of the screen and push. The dashed circle shows where. |
| Run | Push the joystick right to its edge. The ring turns orange and says RUN. A long tap-to-walk trip also runs on its own. |
| Look around | Drag anywhere else on the screen with your right thumb. |
| Walk somewhere | Tap the ground. |
| Talk to someone | Tap the character or their name badge. You walk over and the conversation opens. Or walk up to them and tap the big blue **Talk** button. Chests, map boards and trophy stands show **Open** / **View** instead. |
| Jump | The yellow Jump button (bottom right). |
| Map | 🗺️ at the top right. Pinch to zoom, drag to move, tap a place to set a waypoint. |
| Menu | ☰ at the top right on a phone: Quests, Results, Help, Sign in, and Options. |
| Answer questions | Tap the on-screen number pad, or the choice buttons. |

Touch controls switch on by themselves on phones and iPads. They can be turned on or off in Options; touchscreen laptops and Chromebooks start with them off.

## Challenges on small screens
- The question card fits the screen and scrolls inside itself. Its main button (Let's go / Check / Next) stays pinned at the bottom.
- Phone sideways: the card sits down the right-hand side and the challenge fills the left. Phone upright: the card is at the bottom, and a small pill suggests turning the phone sideways for a bigger view.
- The challenge camera frames its scene into whatever part of the screen the card leaves free, so the scene never hides under the card.
- Where a challenge needed you to tap small 3D objects, the card now offers the same choices as big buttons:
  - Order the Parts (carrots)
  - Crate Packing (group sizes)
  - Milk Splitter (STOPS / REPEATS and the jugs)
  - Trading Post (tags)
  - Round-Up (Pen a cow / Let one out)
  - Weigh Station, Veggie Plot and Snowball Range (nudge buttons)
  - Farm Shop (a ± button, because a phone's decimal keypad has no minus key for a loss)
- On touch screens, keyboard hints ("(Enter)", "press M", "click") are replaced with touch wording.
