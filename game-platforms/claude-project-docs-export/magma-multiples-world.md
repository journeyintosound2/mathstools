# Magma Multiples — volcano world + ten multiplicative challenges

Sixth region of Mills Maths Adventure, reached by a red basalt-arch portal on Number Island at [17, 31]. Region id `magma-multiples`. The land was built 2026-10-02; the ten challenges and the trophy stand were built 2026-10-03. Neither is deployed yet. Checks: 535, all passing (MG1–MG7 for the land, MCF1–MCF4 plus three per challenge for the activities). Full engineering notes are in the source CLAUDE.md under the two "DONE (2026-10-0x) — MAGMA MULTIPLES" sections.

## The land
- About 33,800 m² of land (2.9× Fraction Farm) inside a lava sea. Lava never hurts: touching it bounces you back to the last safe spot.
- The volcano is 36 m tall. A spiral trail climbs it twice; the steep flank slides you back down. The crater is sealed by lip stones and a locked rune gate.
- The lowland has a lava moat, four lava rivers with four different bridges, a ring road, a lava lake with a causeway and an islet, and nine clearings (Tenfold Terraces was added for the challenges).

## The ten challenges (trophy order = recommended order)
Research spine: Siemon's big ideas (arrays → regions → "for each" → proportion; factor-factor-product; from "groups of" to "times as many"), Hurst & Hurrell's connected big ideas, the National Numeracy Learning Progression (MuS5–MuS7), Fosnot & Dolk's models-as-tools, Anghileri's chunking for division, and the additive trap in proportion.

| # | Challenge | Where · host | Idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Column Rectangles | Basalt Columns · Mr. Dawson | Arrays and factor pairs; turned = same rectangle; primes and squares |
| 2 | Crack the Crystal | Obsidian Grove · Ms. Mahoney | Split arrays → area model; grow to the next ten and take away |
| 3 | Tenfold Terraces | Tenfold Terraces · Ms. Ewings | ×10 / ÷10: digits move, the point doesn't (3.5 × 10 ≠ 3.50) |
| 4 | Halve & Double | Geyser Flats · Mr. Heywood | Same tiles, new rectangle: 16 × 25 = 8 × 50 = 4 × 100 |
| 5 | Factor Forge | Ember Forge · Robot | Split ingots into factors, fuse a friendly ten first |
| 6 | Flower Combos | Fire Flower Garden · Trevor | For each: combinations lay themselves out as an array |
| 7 | Cart Tables | Cinder Cones · Steve | Ratio table for multiplying AND dividing (non-unit rates too) |
| 8 | Lava Channels | Lava Lake Islet · Mr. Pearce | Division by chunking into an open array; remainders in context; sharing |
| 9 | Grow the Dragon | Dragon Bones · Ms. Brookes | Times as many; adding the same amount makes a wonky dragon |
| 10 | Strategy Summit | Crater rim · Ms. Bacon | Capstone: pick the easiest of three correct rewrites, then do it |

- Every set is 15 rounds (5 stages × 3), 25 points a round, 375 max. Gold 100%, silver 75%, bronze 50%.
- Bests are saved locally only (`mma-magma-<key>-best`), like the snow world.
- Mills welcomes arrivals at the plaza. The trophy stand is on the plaza's east edge and opens the Magma trophy grid.
