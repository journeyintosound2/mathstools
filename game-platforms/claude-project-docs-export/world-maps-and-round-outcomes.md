# World maps, waypoints and the "slip along the way" rule (2026-10-10)

Built after Jeff played the Emerald Jungle pilot. Not yet deployed.

## 1. One round-outcome rule for every in-world challenge
Applies to all 32 in-world challenges (Fraction Farm, Snowball Sums, Magma Multiples, Emerald Jungle).

- **Final answer right → celebrate (confetti)**, even if something went wrong earlier in the round.
  - Full marks: the slim "✓ … · +25 XP" strip, which moves on by itself.
  - Points lost on the way: a green card. It says "Right answer! There was a slip along the way, so this round earned N of 25 XP", shows the quick way, and waits for Next. The jungle cards also name the slip, e.g. "1 wrong number on the way · 5 new rungs — it can be done in 3".
- **Final answer wrong → feedback** (shake + the quick way), as before.
- **XP banks as points land** (1 point = 1 XP, the same rate as before). The XP bar moves during the set, and quitting part-way keeps what was earned. The end of a set now pays only the coins.
- **Unchanged:**
  - Single-answer farm challenges (Fence, Round-Up, Order the Parts, Crate Packing) — they have no "on the way".
  - The Milk Splitter and the Trading Post still end a round at their first wrong part.

## 2. World maps and waypoints
- **Map boards.** Every world has a painted map board by its arrival: Number Island, Fraction Farm, Snowball Sums, Magma Multiples, Emerald Jungle and the Playground. Walk up to it and press E (or tap it) to open the map.
- **Opening the map elsewhere.** The **M** key opens it anywhere, except during a challenge. Once a waypoint is set, the 🗺 button on the waypoint chip opens it too.
- **The map itself** is full screen in the world's colours, drawn from the real terrain: trails, rivers, buildings and trees.
  - Every challenge is a numbered pin in trophy order, with its medal shown as a ring.
  - It also shows Mills, the trophy stand, the gates, the Playground's nine staff (✓ when the key is earned) and the island's friends.
- **Using it:** move the mouse or trackpad over the map to see places, scroll or pinch to zoom, and drag to move around. The side list shows every place too.
- **Setting a waypoint:** click a place, or any spot you can walk to.
  - A gold beam of light appears in the world, visible over the trees.
  - An arrow at the top of the screen points the way, with the distance.
  - Arriving clears it with a "You made it!" message. The ✕ on the chip also clears it.
- **Mills' welcome** in every world now explains the map board, waypoints and M.

## 3. Jungle signposts fixed
Mr. Pearce was hard to find because of the jungle signposts:
- the hand-set arrows pointed the wrong way;
- the first signpost stood on the trail;
- nothing pointed to Sunny Knoll.

Every arrow now points down the trail that leads to its place. New signs at the glade's east exit and the lagoon junction point the way to Sunny Knoll.
